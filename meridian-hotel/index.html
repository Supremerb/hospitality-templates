The place

Built in 1954. Barely changed since.

The building was a fishermen's guesthouse for forty years before it was anything else. We kept the bones, the timber, and the terrible little bar, and replaced everything that leaked.

There's no lift, no pool, and no television in the rooms. The blowhole is a nine-minute walk north and the penguins come ashore just past it after dusk.

People ask what there is to do here. Honestly — not much. That's rather the point. — Ruth & Anders, owners
