The kitchen

One fire. Everything passes over it.

There's no gas in the kitchen. Every plate that leaves the pass has been near flame — sometimes for eight hours, sometimes for forty seconds. It limits what we can do, which is exactly why we did it.

The menu is written each Wednesday around whatever came off the boats at Triabunna and out of the ground at Richmond.

Cooking over wood is the least forgiving way to do this. You cannot walk away from it, and neither can we. — Marcus Hale, head chef

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Street parking on Hampden Road is free after 6pm. The dining room is accessible; the mezzanine is not. We hold tables for fifteen minutes.

Children are welcome before 7pm. We're happy to cook around most dietaries with a day's notice.
